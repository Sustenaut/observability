## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-07

Commit `eabc2f024939`; release record `pub:sha2-256:3zez3ehezsfgtqin3rkzfxmgfnhnzxsqykebd6whluqnk6is43wq`.

### Changed
- sender_windows and call_rollups are version 2: each reads its rows from {{since}}, a bucket's start, so a rule reads only its lookback

### Added
- The observability command: it reports its version and, for now, nothing else
- Facts: versioned BigQuery queries over a project's call log, sender_windows from its status table and call_rollups from its calls, and `observability facts` to list them or print one's query for the datasets configured
- The uptime probe: every five minutes from GitHub Actions, each target in UPTIME_TARGETS, down after two failed runs in a row, posted to Slack by the Sustenaut bot in the target's own channel or UPTIME_SLACK_CHANNEL
- Rules and the evaluator: eleven versioned YAML rules over the facts, OBSERVABILITY_SENDERS for each project's always-on and on-demand senders, and `observability rules` and `observability evaluate`, which prints the signals starting and ending now
- The evaluator writes its signals to storage's signals tenant when OBSERVABILITY_STORAGE_URL is set, one Avro file a run, and counts those written but not loaded yet as open
- The trends page at observability.sustenaut.eu/trends/integrations, behind the team sign-in: open and recent signals, calls by plugin, sender liveness and page times with cold starts, over the last day
- Each signal has its own page, /signals/<rule_id>/<started>/<subject>, behind the team sign-in: the signal and its evidence, the rule's trend against its threshold, the facts around it, and the rule's other signals for the subject. observability.links makes its address, and rules may have a trend
